# Refund Policy
Customers may request a refund within 30 days of purchase with a valid receipt. Refunds over $500 require manager approval. Approved refunds are processed in 5 to 7 business days to the original payment method. See [[returns-process]] for how to return an item and [[warranty-terms]] for defective products.
