# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy
Employees receive 20 days of paid time off (PTO) per year, accruing 1.67 days per month. Up to 5 unused days carry over into the next year. Requests longer than 3 days need two weeks of notice. Review the [[expense-policy]] for travel related costs.
